We were able to get the SHARC and Scarlett set up for adaptive control. The setup is as follows:

Laptop out -- amp R (source speaker)

error mic -- Scarlett analogue in 1
ref mic -- Scarlett analogue in 2

Scarlett direct monitor 1 and 2 >-- SHARC line in
* Scarlett is set up to make each direct monitor channel mono

SHARC audio out -- amp L (panel)


With this we were able to get a simple passthrough script running properly, 
playing audio from the laptop through the source speaker and the ref mic pickup being played through the panel.
